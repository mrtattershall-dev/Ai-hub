GitHub limits the "About" field to ~350 characters. Pick whichever fits
your taste. They're listed shortest first.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
OPTION 1 — Concise (under 200 chars, fits comfortably)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

HTML5 Canvas port of Kent Dahl's Magic Maze (1994 Turbo Pascal). Faithful
recreation in vanilla JS. Originally inspired by Gauntlet II on the Atari ST.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
OPTION 2 — With history nod (~280 chars)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

A faithful HTML5 Canvas port of Kent Dahl's Magic Maze — a simple, low-tech,
monster-bashing maze game first started on Atari ST in 1990, released for DOS
in 1996, and ported to Ruby in 2004. This is the web version. Vanilla JS,
no build step.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
OPTION 3 — Tagline-led (~210 chars)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

A simple, low-tech, monster-bashing maze game — now in your browser.
HTML5 Canvas port of Kent Dahl's 1996 DOS classic, by his brother. Originally
inspired by Gauntlet II on the Atari ST.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

My recommendation: Option 3. It leads with the official Kent tagline (which
keeps lineage consistent across every port), credits Kent, signals the family
connection, and ends on the Gauntlet II origin. All in 210 characters.
