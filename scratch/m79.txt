Entry 24: lineage closes the renamed-coordinate blind spot, generalizes C2, and meets the architecture's own unforgeability

v2 was frozen first, in its own commit, with its five measured blind spots written down before
anything that might make it look broader in retrospect.

Support is now discovered by execution rather than by matching names, which closes the
derived-by-another-name blind spot for the adapter case: producer and version support the criterion,
document and ordinal support the history, with no name matching anywhere. And aggregation turns out
to be observable by the same means, so C2 becomes a mismatch between observed and declared
aggregation - the earlier weakening invariant needed a human to encode that derive is conjunctive,
and this needs only the operation's own contract.

The entry's finding is that the same measurement is impossible at HEAD. The perturbation builds a
modified copy of a token, and since the C3 repair a token is branded by WeakSet membership, so a
copy is not an authority token and derive correctly refuses it - verified at both commits rather
than inferred. The architecture's own unforgeability defeats external perturbation: the repair made
tokens harder to forge and by the same property harder to screen. Found by the screen failing rather
than by anyone reasoning about it, and the fix is named rather than built.

Three defects in the probe, each found by running it. A refused input read as a dependency,
producing a confident aggregation where nothing had been observed. UNSUPPORTED and ANY_OF conflated,
which are opposite findings. And could-not-measure sharing one representation with was-removed - the
oldest defect class in this project, UNKNOWN collapsing into a value, committed inside the layer
built to detect it. That third one is the most instructive thing in the entry: the layer whose
purpose is to notice information loss lost that very distinction in its own return value, at the
first attempt.
