Preregister the LegaScreen holdout: freeze the selection rule and the outcome vocabulary before any candidate is named

v1's three cases each motivated a probe, so rediscovering them shows the probes were faithfully
built and not that they transfer. A fourth defect, chosen mechanically from a pool none of the
probes were designed around, is the first thing in this line of work that could fail honestly.

Five eligibility conditions, all required: recorded before LegaScreen existed, changed an
authority-relevant decision, has an identifiable live commit and repair commit, is none of the three
pathologies the probes were built around, and is cited in neither screening preregistration.
Selection takes the first identifier in the ledger's own canonical order, with no property of any
candidate inspected before the rule picks.

Two readings are reported because they answer different questions and reporting one would be
choosing the flattering half afterwards. STRICT runs the probes with their committed drivers and
targets exactly as they stand, asking whether the screen as shipped reaches the defect. POINTED runs
the same probe code with the harness aimed at the module the defect lives in, under a driver that
exercises that module's exports in the obvious way and is reproduced verbatim in the result so the
claim can be judged. No probe is added, edited or retuned after selection; if a probe would need to
change, the outcome is SILENT and that is the finding.

Four outcomes fixed now - DETECTED, PARTIAL, SILENT, UNMEASURABLE - with what follows each decided
now so the result cannot choose its own consequence. SILENT is a good result: it measures how narrow
the microscope is, and narrowness stated is worth more than breadth assumed. No probe is written in
this slice under any outcome.

Also recorded before it can be quoted as more: because the C1 repair already closed the ANY route,
that discovery does not show the screen would have prevented a new defect prospectively. It shows
the repair's protected region was larger than the manually demonstrated one and that the screen
could expose that afterwards. Real, and narrower than it sounds.
