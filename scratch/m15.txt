PROVENANCE BINDING. The retirement sidecar keyed provenance by PATHNAME, which is the module:line identity
mistake one layer out: rewrite an artifact and the new bytes silently inherit the old attribution. Since
PROVENANCE.json now participates directly in the evidence chain, its referential integrity can change
entitlement today rather than hypothetically.

    THE PROPERTY, FROZEN BEFORE THE IMPLEMENTATION: provenance attaches to immutable artifact identity,
    not to where the artifact happens to be stored.

A content digest is one implementation of that property, not the property itself. Seven controls, every
expected behaviour decided BEFORE running:

    same path, changed bytes        provenance MUST NOT transfer
    different path, identical bytes provenance IS retained - the admit control, decided in advance,
                                    because moving a file does not change what produced it and without
                                    this "referential integrity" would only mean brittleness
    missing artifact                provenance does not manufacture evidence
    undeclared binding              REFUSED
    modified sidecar                DETECTABLE, via a seal over the ledger
    historical artifact             producer binding reconstructible from content alone
    lifetime                        lookup succeeds with nothing available but bytes and ledger

The live sidecar is migrated: 21 artifacts, 0 unknown, digest-keyed, sealed.

PRODUCER #2 SELECTION FROZEN BEFORE CANDIDATES ARE INSPECTED. Every external claim in r4 rests on ONE
authority, CPython doctest - the narrowest part of the evidence base, and an abstraction validated against
a single producer is an abstraction SHAPED BY IT.

Eligibility is boring and mechanical: an independently implemented external authority already available;
establishing a real software fact Legasus has reason to consume; differing from doctest on AT LEAST THREE
of evidence unit, identity scheme, execution model, native vocabulary, criterion, state/history semantics,
isolation model; machine-readable without reimplementing its semantics. Selection takes the candidate
differing on the MOST axes, ties by lexicographically smallest name - chosen for STRAIN rather than for
expected agreement, and fixed before the list is seen so that distinction is checkable.

E1-E10 frozen. E7 IS THE ANTI-COLLAPSE CONTROL: at least one distinction must remain PRODUCER-SPECIFIC,
because flattening every producer to PASS/FAIL/UNKNOWN would satisfy E1-E6 trivially while abstracting
away the thing the boundary exists to carry.

E10 combines two halves discovered separately - subject lifetime != evidence lifetime, and producer
lifetime != evidence lifetime - into something stronger than "save provenance":

    THE LIFETIME OF JUSTIFIED EVIDENCE IS DETERMINED BY ITS RECORDED VALIDITY CONDITIONS, NOT BY THE
    CONTINUED EXISTENCE OF EITHER ENDPOINT THAT PRODUCED IT.

If producer #2 forces the supposedly generic boundary to change shape, that is THE RESULT - recorded, not
engineered away.
