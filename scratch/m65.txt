LegaScreen v0: the decisive prediction held on one defect, with five artifacts and a named unscreened region

The screen, told nothing about SC-1 and pointed by a driver that composes the module the way
quiesce-check does, flagged stopping.objectivesFromContest for losing establishes and
doesNotEstablish at 77fd921 and does not flag it at HEAD. A mechanical screen with a declared
invariant rediscovered a defect that had taken twenty commits, seven green suites and an owner's
review to find. The sharpest form was not in the preregistration and came out of running it: the
DELTA between two commits' positives is the regression.

False positives are reported as predicted and not tuned away. Four positives at HEAD, six at
77fd921, and most are one artifact worth naming precisely - JavaScript lets a function be called
with the wrong shape without throwing, so feeding a contest to evidenceFrontier yields a plausible
object that lost fields the input happened to carry. The honest tally is ONE principled true
positive and five artifacts, of which two happen to point at functions that really did have SC-3 and
SC-4 defects. Those two are coincidences rather than detections, and counting them as hits would be
the metric-as-property error this project has already paid for twice.

The unscreened region is the point: one of six exported functions was never called, so the run says
nothing whatever about it. A clean positives list without that line would be the 452/452 defect
wearing a lab coat.

The region earned, per protection.mjs, is field-level erasure over one module's reachable exports,
with a witness, covering SC-1 and nothing else. Not covered and stated so: value-level loss inside a
retained field, array and Map-shaped transformations, every other module, every other pathology.
Specificity is limited by the language rather than by the invariant, which a declared signature per
export would fix and which does not exist here. v0 does not license a second slice on enthusiasm.

The worktree method changed mid-run: git worktree at 77fd921 failed on a Windows path-length limit
in an unrelated measurement file, so the single self-contained module was extracted instead.
Recorded because it is why the method is what it is.
