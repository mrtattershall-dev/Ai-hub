Entry 20: give COMPLETE back its subject, and record what LegaScreen v0 actually earned

The owner withdrew an earlier overstatement of their own, and it lands on something I wrote two
hours ago. attainment reported COMPLETE as NOT_REPRESENTABLE with the gloss that no important
failure exists anywhere. That is right about its subject - the failure-class space of arbitrary
unconstrained software, an open-world claim over an unenumerated space - and wrong if read as
completeness being unreachable. Over a bounded contract with a finite input domain, a fully
specified behaviour and no side effects, sixteen states can be checked and there is no philosophical
mystery left.

And this repository already held the authority for that case. GENERALIZATION.EXHAUSTIVE is exactly
the domain was enumerated and all of it was checked, and widen already refuses it without the
enumeration carried as evidence - now asserted by AT-3b, which shows the refusal and then the grant
and then the widened claim being entitled across the domain. The machinery for a PROVE mode is older
than the proposal for one. The refusal now names its subject in the code and in the printed verdict,
and the entry records that perfection is relative to a criterion rather than a property of source,
which is why dominance keeps a frontier instead of electing a winner.

and reported as never called, and a region covering SC-1 and nothing else. The design's remaining
