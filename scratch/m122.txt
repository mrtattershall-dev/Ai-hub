Measure the backdoor detector's 1-of-4: the cause is wrapper indirection, not the threshold, and my explanation was wrong

RESULT.transfer-2.md said three of the four aggregate exports failed the half-the-properties
threshold and that whether they differ in shape or the threshold is too strict was unmeasured.
Measured now, and the threshold has nothing to do with it.

The one that was caught lists its private bindings as bare identifier references. The three that were
missed wrap each binding in an arrow - autoTools returning a new Set, collect calling
collectGodotFiles, brake calling supervisorBrake - so their value nodes are ArrowFunctionExpression
and my filter, which keeps only Identifier values, drops them all. Their property list comes out
EMPTY and the length guard declines before any threshold is consulted. A getter is dropped the same
way.

So the detector recognises re-export by REFERENCE and not re-export by WRAPPER, though both expose
the same module-private binding.

AND THAT IS THE SAME DEFECT AS EVERYTHING ELSE TODAY: an incidental property of the representation,
whether the author wrote a bare reference or an arrow that calls it, was allowed to decide a semantic
classification. It is the readable brand, the name-keyed admission, the consumer-count backdoor rule
and String(v) again - this time inside the detector written to find that family.

NOT FIXED. Widening the rule to count wrapper bodies would be fitting it to the three specimens that
exposed it, and the honest sensitivity number is the one measured before the fix. What changes is
only that the cause is known rather than guessed. A repair needs its own preregistration with a
falsifier and a specimen set not drawn from these four, or the next recall figure measures the
fixture again, which is the finding that started this.
