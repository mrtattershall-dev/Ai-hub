Preregister H-LOSS: three hard criteria, a corpus built for a different hypothesis, and one prospective prediction about my own screen

The hypothesis is the owner's: a substantial fraction of historical failures occur after a richer
distinction was available upstream, discarded by a representation boundary, and subsequently
reconstructed or defaulted downstream. The suspicion underneath is that the recurring problem is not
representation leakage but lossy projection - reality compressed before anyone knows which
distinctions later reasoning will need, with the downstream reconstruction becoming an oracle.

All three criteria must be named separately and concretely: the upstream distinction demonstrably
existed, the boundary demonstrably maps two distinguishable states onto one representation, and an
authority-bearing operation demonstrably infers or defaults the lost distinction. If any one is not
demonstrable the entry is NOT_H_LOSS with no interpretive rescue, and the classifier refuses an entry
that omits any of the three, so the criteria not being relaxed is a measurement rather than a promise.

The corpus is the 31 entries of h-default-corpus.json unchanged, plus the defects named in Entries 31
and 32, enumerated before classification. That corpus was built for a different hypothesis and is
reused precisely because it was not selected for this one.

L-2 is the test against H-LOSS being a bigger word for the same thing. If every H-DEFAULT EXPLAINED
entry is H-LOSS and nothing else is, this is a relabelling; the prediction is that H-LOSS covers
defects H-DEFAULT called NOT_AN_OMISSION and that at least one H-DEFAULT EXPLAINED entry fails a
criterion, and it is refuted if the sets coincide or if H-LOSS turns out to be a superset containing
everything.

L-3 is prospective and is stated before running it. coordinates() projects authority coordinates with
String(v), which is a lossy boundary, and the delta comparison downstream feeds the support formula
and then a contract verdict - so a counterfactual that genuinely changes a coordinate from one
non-primitive value to a different non-primitive value should be reported as NO CHANGE, yielding a
false CONSTANT or ALL_OF. Its strength is rated honestly in the preregistration: a prediction about
unobserved behaviour rather than unseen code, worth less than one about a subject I cannot see and
more than a retrodiction, and not to be upgraded later.

L-4 predicts no repair. This slice changes no production code and no frozen mechanism, and
BACKWARD_1_FROZEN.txt must still verify at the end. TRANSFER-1 is untouched and the hub run remains
unspent - H-LOSS needs its own later experiment rather than a reinterpretation of that one.

The event-graph architecture and the projection-carries-its-loss mechanism are NOT built. Freezing
the hypothesis comes first.
