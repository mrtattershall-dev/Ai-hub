Entry 21: the three-case bar is met, and a generated space reached what hand enumeration missed

The owner's success criterion was frozen before any probe was written, including the definition of
same machinery so it could not be argued afterwards - one harness with pluggable probes, and a probe
needing the defect's file, function, field or commit named in it fails. Met on all three: erasure at
77fd921, alias at 58b62aa, composition at b11e51f, with all three silent at HEAD over the same
spaces.

The decisive prediction was the composition probe, because it is the only one written as a
metamorphic property over a generated space rather than as a scan - lengthening a justification path
must not grant what the direct path refuses - enumerating sixty-four three-node chains and comparing
against the two-node graph with the same endpoints. The erasure result could have been a scan
finding a scan-shaped defect; this one could not.

And it found a route this project never enumerated. An intermediate at ANY launders identically to
one at null. Wave 1 predicted and tested the null case only. The repair happens to close the other,
which is why HEAD is silent, but the repair covering it was luck of construction rather than
coverage demonstrated - which is the distinction Entry 19 exists to keep. First evidence here that a
generated space reaches a member of a class that preregistered hand enumeration missed.

False positives are reported rather than tuned, with the alias set differing across commits, which
is why the delta and not the count is the signal. The screen is itself screened: every probe shown
to fire on a synthetic defective module and be silent on a sound one over the same space, and to
report an unusable target with examined at zero. The region is stated, classCoverage stays UNKNOWN,
and the unbuilt parts of the design are named rather than implied.
