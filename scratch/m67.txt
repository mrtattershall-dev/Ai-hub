Preregister LegaScreen v1: three historical defects, one machinery, before any probe is written

The owner set a failable bar - given a historical commit before the defect was known, the screen
independently surfaces the transition that later became a recorded defect, while preserving explicit
UNSCREENED for what it cannot evaluate, and does it for the objectivesFromContest case, the
provenance reason case and one composition case using the same machinery rather than three special
cases. v0 did the first. This does all three or fails.

Same machinery is defined before it can be argued afterwards: one harness doing enumeration,
exercise, positive collection, coverage accounting and the unscreened report, with pluggable
invariant probes - which is the owner's own plan of expanding one invariant at a time and rescanning.
A probe that needs the defect's file, function, field or commit named in it fails the bar, as does one
whose thresholds were adjusted after seeing its target.

Three probes as general properties. Erasure is v0 unchanged. Alias enumerates state words owned by
more than one module, reports owners and classifies nothing. Composition is the genuinely new one and
is metamorphic: lengthening a justification path must not grant what the direct path refuses,
compared over generated three-node graphs with scopes drawn from a small domain, knowing nothing
about null, about C1 or about which dimension. That is the decisive prediction, because the other two
restate work already done and this is the first probe written as a property over a generated space
rather than as a scan.

Six predictions including silence at HEAD, a coverage and unscreened report on every run, and false
positives reported rather than tuned - the seven standing collisions and the five wrong-shape
artifacts stay in the numbers.

What is not built is named rather than implied: no repository-wide authority graph, no runtime
instrumentation, no static parser, no mutation battery, no shrinking, no repair packets, no severity
dimensions, no bisection beyond the three commits, no per-model rendering. The owner described a
cathedral and asked for a vertical slice; this is the slice.
