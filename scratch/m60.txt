Entry 18: two completeness gaps rather than one, the level actually earned, and this session falsifying its own compounding claim

Entry 17 called the frontier gap the same gap one layer down. It is not. Question completeness - what
investigations have we formulated - and evidence completeness - what producers have we declared
required - are different axes, and neither implies the other. A frontier can be correct over its
questions and its producers while reality contains one more of each, with nothing inside the
computation able to know. CLOSED means closed over the declared requirements, never that the evidence
space is complete.

The level earned is computed against the real entries and is weaker than QUIESCENT sounded:
NO_CURRENT_OBJECTIVE. Frontier exhausted fails on exactly one of eight questions, the UNKNOWN-classed
one, which is neither resolved nor blocked - nothing to do now is not nothing left open. Named
coverage exhausted fails because coverage needs an outside witness and instruments reports the rigs'
classes as UNKNOWN. Complete is NOT_REPRESENTABLE rather than false, since false would imply the
question was evaluated.

And the strong form of the compounding claim is falsified by this session. C4 was repaired at
a31046c; four commits later the C8 repair exported CONTESTED against the ledger's word whose meaning
carries an obligation, and the obligation travelled. C4's guard was producer-keyed promotion inside
one module - it guarded a mechanism while the ledger called it a class. Mechanizing an instance does
not mechanize its class, and recording an instance repair as a class repair is how the next instance
gets four commits of cover. The weak form is what was built: a frozen inventory that would have
fired on the real defect, proven by reconstruction, classifying nothing.

nesting depth, so a green inventory is never read as no collisions anywhere.
