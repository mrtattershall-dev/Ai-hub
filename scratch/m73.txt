Preregister LegaScreen v2: two invariants rather than one, on a witness-mutation-invariant architecture

Recorded before any code, so it cannot look like a convenient discovery later: the owner's general
formulation - output authority must have ancestry in the inputs - does NOT catch the holdout. C2 was
derive of an empty context with a repository context yielding that repository, and S1 is in the
closure of the inputs because premise two established it. Nothing was minted from nothing. The
defect is that derive is conjunctive, its own source saying the output context is the intersection,
and it took a dimension only one premise established. So v2 implements two invariants: ancestry,
universal, strip a dimension from every input and the output must not establish it; and weakening,
for operations whose own documentation declares conjunctive combination, strip it from one input and
the output must not establish it. The owner's instruction not to collapse single-edge minting and
multi-edge laundering into one graph invariant is obeyed - the data has not earned that reduction.

The architecture correction the holdout forced: pointed at calculus, the erasure probe produced
seven positives over eight functions, nearly all because a wrong-shape call returned a plausible
object, so the screen could not tell semantic abnormality from nonsense in and nonsense out. That is
LegaExercise's own discipline arriving at the screening layer - witness.mjs has said since r2 that
there is no observation without execution, and v0 and v1 ignored it. Every v2 probe now has three
parts: an opportunity that must be a call which actually succeeds, one controlled perturbation of
that witnessed call, and an invariant; a probe that cannot complete part one reports unscreened.

Five predictions including one of NO detection, a positive control so that discarding every
dimension cannot pass, and a specificity requirement that v2 produce fewer positives on calculus
than the erasure probe's seven of eight or the witness requirement bought nothing.

The only result worth reporting as new is the rescan. Catching C2 is a regression test with extra
steps because C2 created the probe. The question is what it finds besides C2 across every legaknow
module at HEAD and three historical trees, with four conditions for counting a finding as new, and
an explicit falsification: if the rescan surfaces only C2 and artifacts, v2 is a regression test
wearing a screen's clothes and no probe is added to chase a better result.
