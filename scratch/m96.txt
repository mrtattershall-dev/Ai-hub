BRIDGE-1: the bridge manufactured 965 findings against working production code, and B-4b failed

Production is justification.covers and the specification is calculus.delegate. Production does not
import the calculus and does not know it exists. 12,301 production calls were witnessed from existing
tests and each was put to the specification through the specification's own constructors, with both
endpoints pinned by digest.

B-6 held: zero RESULT_DISAGREEMENT and zero REASON_DISAGREEMENT. The wide bridge declares PARTIAL and
therefore licenses no verdict at all, so all 12,301 comparisons are UNMAPPABLE. The restricted bridge
declares EQUIVALENT on the stated domain where both quantifiers range over the same dimension set,
and there 770 AGREE and 3,990 are UNMAPPABLE. A large UNMAPPABLE count is the honest expected result
rather than a failure of the slice.

BUT THE BRIDGE MANUFACTURED 965 FINDINGS AGAINST PRODUCTION, TWICE. The first run reported 965
RESULT_DISAGREEMENTs, every one the same shape: covers treats ANY as licensing anything and the
calculus context has no wildcard at all, since delegate compares contexts with strict inequality. The
second run left 4, and two of those had granted and required IDENTICAL while the specification still
refused, because the scopes carried an UNADMITTED object and strict inequality over a nested object
decides on reference identity rather than on worlds. Production was right both times, the
specification was right both times, and a mistaken correspondence produced accusations against
working code. The repair is general rather than per-case: a non-primitive scope value has no faithful
counterpart. Stripping UNADMITTED before translating was REJECTED, because that would be the bridge
claiming the drop is meaning-preserving.

AND B-4b FAILED. The preregistration said a corrupted bridge must not produce silent agreement. It
does. With production correct and the specification correct and refusing for genuinely different
reasons, one edited line of reason_correspondence turns REASON_DISAGREEMENT into AGREE, and nothing
in the comparison can tell. The bridge is the oracle for its own correctness, and provenance plus
endpoint pinning give attributability and staleness rather than detection. A test asserts the failure
so it cannot quietly become behaviour, and the two mutation modes that DO work - implementation and
specification - are asserted beside it.

What would be needed is not more provenance but a second independently derived correspondence to
disagree with the first, or reason classes derived from counterfactual behaviour rather than
declared. Neither is built and neither is claimed.

The calculus is still not wired into production. Wiring them would let both sides share one
implementation defect and agreement would stop being evidence.
