Preregister SEMANTIC-1: two tracks kept apart, a contract that goes stale when its subject moves, and no wiring of the calculus into production

SURFACE-1 established that production paths consuming the authority calculus number zero, which
settles that the repository is case B - the calculus is an executable specification rather than the
production authority substrate. This slice does not touch that. Wiring the calculus into production
to improve the screen would contaminate the architecture and would build software that is easy for
its own scanner to read rather than a scanner that reads software.

The three roles are discovered rather than named: a call that consumed no authority and produced
some is a PRODUCER, one that consumed authority and produced none is a CONSUMER, one that did both is
a TRANSDUCER, all decided by the subject's own brand predicate. The two tracks share the AUTO-CF-1
experiment and differ only in what is read off the end, so the observer becomes a parameter - support
coordinates for Track P, the output value itself for Track C. They are kept separate and no claim is
made that they are one invariant.

Track P infers a support formula from counterfactual cells with a deliberately tiny vocabulary,
ALL_OF, ANY_OF, NTH, CONSTANT and UNKNOWN, where UNKNOWN is always available and is never a failure
state. P-3 is the mechanical anti-self-ratification rule: a contract pins to a digest of its subject,
so editing the implementation invalidates the criterion by construction and a stale contract can
neither convict nor absolve. P-4 says an observed relation with no established contract is recorded
as CHARACTERIZED, because the screen does not invent requirements.

P-5 is the milestone the owner set: at least one mechanically discovered transform going all the way
from DISCOVERED to SCREENED with no hand-written driver. It is preregistered with the qualification
that the CONTRACT remains human testimony - what disappears is the driver, not the criterion.

C-2 is the control that stops always-refuse from winning: at least one consumer case where a
legitimately weakened authority input must leave the decision UNCHANGED because the removed
coordinate is irrelevant. Without it a screen that convicts every perturbation would look perfect.

S-8 makes module-resolution completeness an explicit coordinate rather than an absence, so a dynamic
import acorn cannot resolve reads UNRESOLVED and the report looks gray rather than green.

Backward discovery from effect sinks is named as NOT in this slice, so the surface stays
one-directional and CAVEAT still stands.
