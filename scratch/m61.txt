Preregister the protection levels: a named pattern, a demonstrated region and a covered class are three different claims

The owner, reviewing Entry 18, named three things this project has been conflating - a failure
pattern is conceptual similarity across incidents, a mechanized region is the exact area where a
detector has demonstrated reach, and a covered class needs independently justified coverage. The
ledger has been writing mechanized for the second and reading it as the third, which is how C4 got
four commits of cover, and at scale is how a catalogue of named failures starts to look like a
guarantee.

Measured before predicting: twenty commits from introducing the CONTESTED incident to its being
recorded as a defect, seven of them reporting a passing full suite, and collectionCohort is not an
exported state word anywhere - so the detector built for the second incident could not have seen the
first, which was a runtime data key colliding with a dimension name rather than a cross-module
export.

Six predictions. The two detectors are disjoint over the two incidents, each covering exactly one,
which is the fact that makes the overclaim visible. The verdict should be MECHANIZED_REGION even
though the union covers two of two known incidents, because covering every observed member is not a
coverage argument over the class, and observed coverage belongs beside the verdict rather than as
the verdict. COVERED_CLASS needs coverage established outside the detectors' own say-so. Controls
for an unwitnessed claim, for a pattern that genuinely does reach COVERED_CLASS, and for one with no
detector at all. The detection-distance figure is reported as one before-and-after on one incident
and nothing more.

Not done, and stated so: history is not rewritten - entries 15 to 18 keep the word they used, and
the distinction applies to what is claimed from here. No long-run metric framework is built over a
single data point.
