Repair C9-b: the TERMINAL verdict is measured on the recorded bytes of external.json, not on whatever occupies its path

The quiesce check classified REPOC_UNRESOLVED_ATTRIBUTION as TERMINAL by reading
benchmarks/repoC/external.json by path and asking whether its records carry a disambiguating
coordinate. Regenerated with richer records under the same name, that read would have flipped the
entry to JUSTIFIED and the fresh observations would have silently replaced the old evidence rather
than resolved it - the exact thing Entry 10 says cannot happen, made to happen by a file that kept
its name. Same path is not same referent.

legaknow/pin.mjs pins a measurement to the digest PROVENANCE.json recorded for the artifact and
returns the bytes only when they match; REPLACED, ABSENT and UNPINNED re-measure nothing and say
why. The quiesce check reads through it and prints the identity line first. Run at this commit:
PINNED fc0f04acdfd9 against recorded fc0f04acdfd9, fields unchanged, three conflicting keys, no
discriminator, QUIESCENT_CONTEST with zero objectives - the verdict is what it was, now about the
bytes it was always about.

Stated rather than implied: the REPLACED branch of the quiesce check itself was not exercised on the
repository artifact, because that would mean rewriting it. The branch is exercised in pin.test on
temporary files under the same function the check calls; the check's own line shows the function ran
on the real artifact.
