Record the repeat/budget preregistration draft, unauthorized and unrun

Left untracked at the end of the last session, which is exactly how a record gets lost. It states
that the replay rig CANNOT test a budget increase - the mock cannot react, so extra turns surface as
EXHAUSTED, an artefact of the instrument rather than a measurement of the hub - and splits the
question accordingly: offline measures whether a repeat policy RECLAIMS CALLS, live measures whether
reclaimed calls PRODUCE WORK and at what cost. The offline arm is a precondition, not a result.

Denominator measured before proposing anything: across 1213 scenarios and 17466 recorded replies,
10.3 percent of replies are byte-identical repeats of an earlier reply in the same run, rising to 51.3
percent in the setH 14B control arm and 46.4 percent in setG's. The weaker signal, same tool and path
with an identical body at 34.8 percent, is deliberately not leaned on because re-reading a file that
has since changed is legitimate.

Endpoints are fixed in the document, including cost per goal COMPLETED rather than completion alone,
and reach kept separate from completion because conversion has held near 73 percent while reach moved.
The null is preregistered: reclaiming calls does not by itself raise completion. EXHAUSTED and the
status distribution are the guards against a policy that reclaims calls by truncating runs, which
would look like efficiency and be a loss.

Nothing in the document authorizes a run. The live arm is a spend decision.
