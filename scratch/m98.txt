Preregister H-DEFAULT: freeze the role table before looking at a single defect, and state the prospective prediction first

The hypothesis is the owner's: historically distinct authority defects can be reproduced as
incorrect role-specific completion of an omitted semantic coordinate. The claim underneath is that an
empty object has no single correct reading - as a query it means I am not asking, as a delta it means
preserve, as evidence it means not established, as a state it means incomplete - and that ANY is none
of those because it is an explicit value.

The six roles and their six completion laws are frozen in this commit, taken verbatim, and the hard
failure condition is stated before the data: if the taxonomy needs a new role, a new completion law,
or a per-defect special case, then H-DEFAULT is just another explanation machine and will be reported
as refuted. Six roles in, six roles out. The corpus rule is also fixed in advance - every defect
named in ledger Entries 15 through 30, whether or not it looks like an omission problem, so the
denominator includes the ones the hypothesis cannot touch.

D-3 is the prospective test and is stated before any classification has been done. delegate receives
context in the REQUEST role, whose completion law is INHERIT, and the current implementation inherits

table both are REQUEST-role omissions and both should inherit. I predict the inconsistency is real
and was not deliberate, and further that it is not a safety defect - refusing never grants more than
inheriting - but an expressiveness one. If the difference turns out to be deliberate and documented,
D-3 is refuted, and saying so now is what stops that being adjusted later.
