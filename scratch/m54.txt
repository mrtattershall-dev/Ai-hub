Preregister the consumption question: does the completeness bound survive the artifacts derived from a verdict?

The owner, reviewing Entry 16, said the qualification has to survive consumption - a consumer that
reads only the state and discards doesNotEstablish recreates the information-loss problem at the API
boundary - and said they had not inspected it. This inspects it, and finds three sites rather than
one.

objectivesFromContest takes the whole verdict and returns objectives and a why, with neither bound
field, which means the bound is erased at exactly the step that turns a verdict into
work-or-no-work, and that artifact is what PURPOSE consumes. nextAction makes the same shape of
claim - there is currently no justified next action - over a candidate list someone supplied, and
never had a bound at all. And the third, which the owner did not name: evidenceFrontier's CLOSED
means every producer on a DECLARED list was attempted, its why reads as completeness over the
evidence, and quiesce-check declares that list by hand. The gap Entry 16 named at the contest layer
is already present one layer below it, and inherited upward.

Five predictions. SC-2 puts the project's own Law 1 instrument on its own new API:
informationMonotonicity with the verdict as the rich state and objectivesFromContest as the erasure
should report a forbidden transition, permission gained by information loss, with a non-vacuity
requirement that the same consumer is refused on the rich state. The expected repair gives each
artifact the bound appropriate to its own claim, since the three claims differ. The residual is
stated in advance: carrying the bound makes dropping it deliberate rather than automatic; it does
not make it inseparable, and a representation where the state cannot be read alone is not started.
