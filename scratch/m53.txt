that no objective FORMULATED in the frontier is justified; it does not establish that no justified
objective exists. The frontier can be perfectly correct over the questions it holds while being
incomplete over the questions that could be asked, and there may be no complete solution to the
second problem - the defect was never saying which one the law reaches.

Mechanized rather than commented, because a bounded claim quoted without its bound is an unbounded
claim: every contestState result carries establishes and doesNotEstablish, and quiesce-check prints

QUIESCENT with zero objectives, then three preregistered waves finding twenty-one authority defects
in deciding paths, each after the previous went green. It adds no objective; a test asserts the
qualification changed no state and no objective it qualifies, and that asking about completeness
generates nothing.
