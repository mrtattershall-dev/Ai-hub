Preregister LegaScreen v3 slice 1: discover the support structure counterfactually instead of matching names

v2's two structural failures - the same-name perturbation assumption and the derived-by-another-name
blind spot - have one cause: it compares field names and has no idea what supports what. This slice
builds that layer and nothing else. The owner's v3 design has twelve parts and a readiness gate;
this preregisters one, because the whole point of the holdout was that breadth asserted is
worthless.

The idea is to take a witnessed call, perturb one input coordinate, re-run and diff the output, and
build an empirical dependency matrix from execution rather than from names. The same machinery
answers what defeated the ancestry invariant, because aggregation is observable too: if removing one
input leaves an output dimension intact the operation behaves disjunctively for it, and if the
dimension disappears it behaves conjunctively.

Five predictions. The matrix should resolve the three dimensions v2 reports as unscreened, showing
criterion depending on producer and history on document and ordinal. A sound derive should observe
ALL_OF. The decisive one is that a defective derive should observe ANY_OF, making C2 a mismatch
between observed aggregation and declared semantics, discovered without any probe being told that
derive is conjunctive - which would mean the hand-authored declaration is no longer what finds C2.
Four-state outcomes become centralized so that a perturbation changing nothing is a first-class
state carrying zero evidence rather than an assertion buried in one function, which is how v2
shipped the bug it later caught. And non-vacuity: the matrix must contain both dependence and
independence, since a matrix saying everything depends on everything has discovered nothing.

Falsification is stated both ways: if the matrix cannot separate derived-from-a-renamed-input from
minted-from-nothing, counterfactual lineage does not close the blind spot and the later parts do not
proceed on a hope; if the aggregation prediction fails, the claim that lineage generalizes C2 is
withdrawn and the declaration stays hand-authored.
