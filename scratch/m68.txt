LegaScreen v1: the three-case bar is met by one harness, and the composition probe found a route my hand analysis missed

Three probes on one harness, run unchanged against HEAD and three historical trees. At 77fd921
P-ERASURE surfaced objectivesFromContest losing establishes and doesNotEstablish. At 58b62aa P-ALIAS
surfaced CONTESTED owned by ledger.mjs and provenance.mjs. At b11e51f P-COMPOSITION surfaced the
laundering chain. At HEAD all three are silent on those findings. No probe names a file, a function,
a field or a commit.

The decisive one is P-COMPOSITION, because it is the only probe written as a metamorphic property
over a generated space rather than as a scan: lengthening a justification path must not grant what
the direct path refuses. It enumerates sixty-four three-node chains, builds the two-node graph with
the same endpoints, and compares entitlement, containing no mention of null, of C1, of which
dimension matters, or of what a laundering defect looks like. So the erasure result was not a scan
finding a scan-shaped defect.

AND IT FOUND A ROUTE I NEVER ENUMERATED. The wave-1 preregistration predicted the null intermediate
and tested exactly that; an intermediate at ANY launders identically and I never wrote it down. The
C1 repair happens to close it, which is why HEAD is silent, but the repair covering it was luck of
construction rather than coverage I had demonstrated - and per protection.mjs that distinction is
the whole point. A generated space reached a member of the class that hand enumeration missed, which
is the first evidence here that screening goes where preregistered attacks did not.

False positives are reported and not tuned: alias carries its standing collisions at every commit,
and a different seven at 77fd921 than at HEAD because pin.mjs exists there and instruments.mjs does
not, which is why the count alone is not the signal and the delta across commits is. Erasure carries
the wrong-shape artifacts whose cause was named in v0.

The probes are themselves screened: each is shown to fire on a synthetic defective module and to be
silent on a sound one over the same sixty-four chains, and to report an unusable target as
unscreened with examined at zero, so silence can never be read as a clean result.
