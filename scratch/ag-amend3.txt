Amendment 3 plus the B-prime regression suite: four corrections, and the six held bindings pinned before anything is built

AMENDMENT 3 corrects the INTERPRETATION of amendment 2's baseline. The measurements stand; three readings
did not.

1 THE EVIDENTIAL BOUNDARY OF G3. I reported "context pins fixture.js - commit edit OTHER.js - COMMITTED".
  What was observed is an IN-PROCESS call to commit() returning committed:true for a mismatched action
  DESCRIPTION. commit() is a pure authorization function and writes nothing. ESTABLISHED: a calculus-level
  authorization failure. NOT ESTABLISHED: that a real write to OTHER.js occurred or could. The execution
  test must connect the two, and until it does G3 is a governance hole rather than a demonstrated
  unauthorized write.

2 G3 IS AN UNCHECKED TARGET BINDING, NOT TIME-OF-CHECK-TO-TIME-OF-USE. I called it TOCTOU; wrong
  mechanism. Unchecked binding: the target is NEVER compared, at any time. TOCTOU: the target IS checked
  and then CHANGES before execution. Fixing the first does not address the second, and conflating them
  would let a correct G3 fix be reported as covering a case never tested. The race is now E9, separate.

3 G1 AND G2 ARE GAPS ONLY AGAINST A DECLARED POLICY. G1 shows DUPLICATED GRANTS, not doubled spending
  authority - sharing a permission without consuming it is ordinary, and it becomes a budget violation
  only where a parent holds a FINITE ALLOWANCE that descendants collectively exceed. G2 is a defect only
  under a DECLARED single-use policy; most permissions are not tickets. Amendment 2 treated both as
  defects on their face. E4 and E5 are therefore conditional on this experiment declaring those policies.

4 THE ENFORCEMENT POINT MUST OWN A STRUCTURED ACTION AND THE ACTUAL EFFECT, and the baseline probe shows
  the trap: it passed action: 'edit OTHER.js' AS A STRING. An enforcement point that validates a string
  while another argument determines the file RECREATES THE HOLE WITH A CHECK IN FRONT OF IT. Immediately
  before invoking the bounded operation it must own the resolved target, the revision, the evidence
  obligations THE OPERATION CONTRACT DECLARES, grant status and remaining allowance - and that same
  structure must be what the operation consumes. G4 is likewise scoped to the contract rather than made a
  universal precondition.

E0 IS NOW THE STRONGEST TEST: an owner-issued grant permits a REAL change to the intended fixture;
changing ONLY the target causes refusal; and BOTH FILES REMAIN BYTE-IDENTICAL to their pre-attempt state.
Byte-identity is what makes it a real-effect test rather than a verdict test. Then one dimension varied at
a time, every negative case retaining an otherwise-valid setup.

THE B-PRIME REGRESSION SUITE IS BUILT AND PASSING, 12/12, before any enforcement exists. It pins the six
bindings the calculus already holds - grant non-widening, context non-dropping, context non-changing, no
belief laundering, no bare epistemic commit, no uncovered grant - plus B7'. The four measured gaps are
pinned as CURRENT BEHAVIOUR so the E-series inverting them is visible as a change, each labelled with what
it would take to be a defect rather than asserted to be one.

B6' IS THE ONE MOST AT RISK AND IT IS NOT A REFUSAL: a controller HOLDING owner-issued authority must stay
exercisable. My amendment 1 called that a regression, which would have prohibited the legitimate case.
controller-HELD is not controller-CREATED, and B6' exists so a future tightening cannot quietly forbid
exercising a legitimate delegation.

No validation borrowed from the 7B fuzzer, which remains untouched at 12 node processes.
