Preregister LegaScreen v0: can a mechanical screen rediscover a defect it was never told about?

The owner proposed a high-sensitivity screen over authority-bearing transformations that tolerates
false positives and feeds narrow diagnostics. The vision is large, and building it from the design
downward would be this project's own worst pattern - machinery whose coverage is asserted rather
than demonstrated, which Entry 19 just finished naming. So v0 tests the single premise everything
rests on: a screen given only the module surface and a declared invariant, told nothing about any
specific defect, flags a transformation this session found by hand.

The pathology is the one with the most recorded instances and the cleanest signature - information
disappears and authority increases - whose Law 1 instrument already exists and has always been
pointed by hand. The screen points it automatically. The authority-relevant field vocabulary is
declared from the laws before the run and not tuned afterwards.

Ground truth is pinned to commits where the defect was live. The decisive prediction is that at
77fd921 the screen flags objectivesFromContest for losing its two bound fields, with no knowledge of
SC-1 or of the preregistration that found it; and that at HEAD the same function is not flagged, so
the screen tracks the repair rather than the name. Non-vacuity requires a countable number of
exercised pairs. False positives are expected and reported as a rate with no threshold tuned to
flatter it. The unscreened region is reported, because not flagged must be distinguishable from
never called - the 452/452 problem applied to the screen itself.

The region v0 will have earned is stated in advance as whatever it flags and nothing more, with its
blind spots named now: value-level loss inside a retained field, losses through array or Map-shaped
transformations, modules outside the scanned set, and every pathology other than erasure. If the
decisive prediction fails, mechanical enumeration plus a declared invariant does not reach what hand
analysis reaches, and the thesis needs a different mechanism rather than a bigger seed corpus bolted
on after seeing the result.
