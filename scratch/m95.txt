Preregister BRIDGE-1: compare production behaviour to the calculus as a specification, and treat the correspondence itself as something that can be wrong

The differential architecture arrived while SEMANTIC-1 was mid-flight. Folding it into a frozen
preregistration would be reinterpreting a prediction after seeing a result, so SEMANTIC-1 was
finished as written and this is preregistered on its own.

The triangle is production behaviour, the calculus as executable specification, and bridge evidence
saying why those refer to the same semantic question. Three objects, and if any edge is missing the
answer is UNKNOWN or UNMAPPABLE rather than a verdict. The calculus is NOT imported into production
and production need not know it exists, because a scanner that requires its subject to adopt its
ontology is a scanner for cooperative software only.

B-1 predicts four comparison states with REASON_DISAGREEMENT kept out of AGREE, and a control that
must fire on a fixture where production and specification give the same answer for different reasons.
A path can refuse safely today for a reason that becomes dangerous downstream, and prod equals spec
cannot see that.

B-4 is the one that will bite. Three independent attack modes are preregistered - implementation
mutation, specification mutation and bridge mutation - and the bridge control must test the dangerous
direction specifically: with production correct and specification correct, a corrupted bridge must
not produce silent AGREEMENT. False agreement is the hazard, not false discrepancy.

B-6 is a prediction of NO detection. Production does not consume the calculus, so the comparison will
mostly be UNMAPPABLE, and a large UNMAPPABLE count is the expected honest result rather than a
failure of the slice to be quietly narrowed into agreement.
