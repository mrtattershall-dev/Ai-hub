proposition depends on, and should not preserve one it does not - with two error directions,
illegitimate collapse and illegitimate separation. NO INVENTION IS CLAIMED: this is contextual and
observational equivalence together with intervention-based identification of relevant variables,
reached from a different direction, and the prior art is older and better developed. What is being
tested is whether the framing predicts something about THIS system that was not already known.

is fixed here before anything is classified: a collapse or separation may be located only in a
PROJECTION, a step that produces a representation consumed downstream, and never in a consumer's
decision procedure. A consumer that had the distinction available and failed to branch on it is
NEITHER. Without that rule every defect can be narrated as a consumer treating two cases alike.
