H-DISTINCTION: DX-1 fired, DX-2 survived by ONE entry, and the descent has a measurable problem

DX-1 tested MINIMALITY, the direction nothing had tested, and it fired. semantic() assigns a fresh
identity to every non-plain object and replay() compares projections to decide baseline stability, so
a subject that is deterministic in every respect anyone could care about is declared
BASELINE_UNSTABLE purely because its output carries a freshly allocated Map. The instrument's
limitation is reported as a property of the subject, and any subject whose outputs hold a Map, Set,
Buffer, stream or class instance is unscreenable by that path and told it is non-deterministic. It is
the opposite error from L-3: an over-fine projection producing a FALSE REFUSAL where String(v)
produced a FALSE AGREEMENT. run-backward does not use replay(), so the frozen hub transfer is
unaffected.

DX-2 SURVIVED BY A SINGLE ENTRY. Thirty-three collapses, two separations, one NEITHER - W3-e, where
the token KIND was carried, distinct and readable at every step and commit() simply did not branch on
it. Under the locating rule, fixed in advance precisely so this could not be argued away, a consumer
failing to consult is not a collapse. Without that rule it would have been 36 of 36.

DX-3 held with two separations, so distinction is not merely H-LOSS relabelled - it names an error
direction H-LOSS could not express - but two out of thirty-six is thin.

THE MEASURABLE PROBLEM WITH THE DESCENT is the trend across three hypotheses on one corpus:
H-DEFAULT covers 42 percent with a discriminating set of 15, H-LOSS covers 83 percent with 6, and
H-DISTINCTION covers 97 percent with 1. Each level down explains more and refutes less, which is the
signature of a frame becoming unfalsifiable rather than becoming true. Reading those coverage numbers
as confirmation would be the exact error every one of these hypotheses was written to detect.

WHAT ACTUALLY PAID IS THE PROSPECTIVE RECORD, WHICH IS 3 OF 3. D-3 found that delegate inherits on an
omitted argument and refuses on an empty object, decided by a falsiness test. L-3 found that String(v)
turns a real change into a false CONSTANT. DX-1 found a deterministic subject declared unstable.
Three hypotheses, three prospective hits, and ZERO defects found by classification. The
classifications produced coverage percentages; the predictions produced defects. That asymmetry
suggests running the ladder by demanding a prospective prediction per level and ignoring the
retrospective fit entirely.

DX-4 held: no production code changed, the freeze still verifies, both L-3's String(v) and DX-1's
