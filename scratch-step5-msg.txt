STEP 5 preregistration: authority continuity across a controller boundary, before any mechanism

This commit contains the preregistration and nothing else.

The owner's method instruction shapes the whole document: do not assume inheritance is the correct
mechanism, first specify the authority proposition that must remain true across the boundary, then see
what representation is actually required to preserve it. So the proposition comes first, the
requirements are derived from it, five candidate representations are enumerated and NONE is chosen, and
the worlds are frozen so reality eliminates them rather than argument.

THE PROPOSITION, decomposed into six refutable properties: independent root, where every authority
traces to an owner-issued grant and no link is derived from model-authored text; non-amplification,
where the parent's authority is a ceiling at any depth; least authority, where the child's set is a
SUBSET and not a copy; non-loss, where a child can do work the parent was authorized for and the parent
survives the child's return; accountability, where an outside reader can recompute from the record
alone which grant authorized each effect and where it was attenuated; and revocation response, with no
stale retention across the boundary.

AND THE TENSION THAT DECIDES THE REPRESENTATION. Least authority requires something to name what the
child needs. The owner cannot know work the controller has not yet discovered, and the model's output
is forbidden as a source of authority - so if neither can name it, least authority is unreachable and
inheritance wins by default, which is where the baseline already is. The candidate escape is stated as
a HYPOTHESIS: attenuation is not amplification, because a requester that can only SHRINK what it passes
on cannot gain anything by lying, so a model-chosen narrowing would violate neither property. Whether
that holds is the experiment, and the most likely reason it would not is composition.

EIGHT FROZEN WORLDS - the owner's five plus three the measured baseline makes necessary. W6 is a
grandchild re-requesting what the child narrowed away, and it is the one I expect to be hardest: if
attenuation is implemented by overwriting shared state a grandchild may re-widen back to the ceiling and
narrowing becomes a suggestion, while if it replaces the set then W7 breaks instead and the parent
permanently loses what the child narrowed away. A representation passing W3 while failing W6 or W7 has
moved the defect rather than fixed it. W8 is accountability, which is not bookkeeping: the baseline
already contains a refusal that happened and left no trace, and an unrecorded correct decision is
indistinguishable from an unrecorded wrong one.

THE REFUSE-EVERYTHING CANDIDATE IS LISTED ON PURPOSE. Representation (e), children refuse all writes,
satisfies every property except non-loss and passes every refusal-shaped test, so any result that does
not distinguish its candidate from (e) has measured nothing. Four must-fire controls follow from that: a
positive control, a specificity control with governance off, an independence control requiring the
frozen widening result to survive step 5 unchanged, and a DEPTH control, because SUBTASK_MAX_DEPTH is 2
and if depth cannot reach a grandchild write then W6 is vacuous. That last is a live risk rather than a
hypothetical - this repo already has a recorded case of a reproduction blocked by a different guard
reporting green forever - so it is checked FIRST and the choice recorded before any result.

NOT DONE HERE. No representation is chosen, no numerical result is predicted, the flag stays off, and
authority lineage is deliberately NOT combined with assumption lineage: those are two different reasons
an action can lose its right to proceed, and combining them now would be doing it because the diagram is
elegant rather than because a measurement forced it. If step 5 produces a case that authority alone
cannot decide, that is the evidence for combining them. Queue and supervisor runs stay out of scope;
spawn_subtask is chosen as the smallest boundary already present and isolable.
