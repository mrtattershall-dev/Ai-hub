  TOTAL refused 28   PROVE would catch 25   PROVE would MISS 0   unassemblable 3   held out as truncated 0

  MISS means the envelope discarded a semantically correct program. CATCH means it merely
  saved the verifier a run. UNASSEMBLABLE means it caught something the verifier could not
  have been pointed at. Those are three different justifications and only one of them is
  a defect.
