FANTASY RPG ICON PACK (by Franuka) v2.3
-------------------------
Thank you for purchasing this asset pack!
It was created from scratch, and licensed under the Creative commons BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
Feel free to use it on any commercial or non-commercial project, just remember to add a link to my itch.io page and/or X, it helps a lot :)
---
Included are all the files for each icon, in .png format, transparent background.
The icons are 16x16px, and upscaled to 32x32 and 48x48
---
All art was made from scratch by me.

CHANGELOG
----------
v1.1: First expansion, added colour variations (95 new icons)
v1.2: Second expansion, fishing set (rods, bait, junk, and a lot of new fishes)
v1.3: Added 10 more defense items (blue robe, shirt, copper armour, shields, etc.)
v1.4: Added exclamation/question marks and math symbols
v1.5: Added mail/letter icon
v1.6: Added 20 extra icons (scrolls, letters, writing material, etc.)
v1.7: Added 20 extra icons (awards, coins, lock, clock, compass, paw, sound, and more)
v1.8: Added 20 extra icons (tickets, lute, flute, drum, concertina, violin, vynil, music sheet)
v1.9: Added 3 ingots (copper, silver, gold)
v1.9.5: Added 10 crops and fruits
v2.0: Spells Expansion. Added 100 new spell icons (10 schools of magic, 10 icons each), plus no frame variations.
v2.1: 20 new Gathering/Outdoors icons, plus a Planks icon (318), and Exit icon (319). Also rearranged the main sheet with these additions.
v2.2: 15 new icons. 4 Elemental swords (ice, earth, fire, lightning), 6 Watering cans (copper, steel, gold), and 5 resource icons (seeds, cotton, yarn, bread, chicken).
v2.3: Fourth expansion, Minecraft icon set! 385 icons, covers all items up to Java Edition 1.11 (Resource pack format 3). If enough people show interest I might keep adding all items from the newest versions, but it's just too much for now :)
