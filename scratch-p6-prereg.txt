P6 preregistration: does previously granted authority remain valid over time?

Frozen before implementation; this commit contains nothing else. Step 6 is closed at 89c0faae and
nothing here revises it.

WHY THIS IS A DIFFERENT QUESTION. Steps 5 and 6 both established continuity and neither touched
validity: queue_task takes parent authority to attenuated descendant authority, repair takes
failed-work authority to derived continuation authority, and in both the continuation mechanism creates
no new authority. R6 destroyed the source and the system refused with SOURCE_ITEM_GONE, which
established that source EXISTENCE is required - not that an existing source is still ENTITLED to
authorize anything. The hostile world: owner grants {a}, F receives it, F fails, a repair references F,
the original authority becomes invalid, F REMAINS PRESENT carrying its historical {a}, and the repair
begins. Step 6 answers that with source exists therefore derive {a}. P6 asks whether that is
legitimate.

THE PROPOSITION. Authority that was validly granted must not remain usable after what entitled it has
ceased to be valid, and a continuation must not silently detach from what justified it.

AND THE FIRST CONTROL IS REACHABILITY, preregistered as step one because the last two frozen worlds
were both vacuous by construction - W6 at spawn_subtask and R7 at repair depth - and both were found by
asking reachability first. Measured here before writing anything: E1 revision binding IS implemented
but fires only when an authority pins a revision, and issueWriteScope pins none, so E1 can never fire
for any authority this slice issues. E4 single use, E5 allowance, E6 expiry, E7 ancestor revocation and
E8 re-delegation are all unimplemented by governed-edit's own header. And there is no explicit
revocation mechanism anywhere - nothing marks a grant, a crossing record or a work item revoked.

So the honest starting position is that there is currently NO WAY for an authority in this system to
become invalid. The proposition is not merely unproven, it is presently UNREPRESENTABLE, and that
statement is P6's first result rather than a pass or a fail. The consequence is frozen now because it
will be tempting to skip: P6 cannot be tested without introducing a representation of invalidity, and
CHOOSING that representation is the substance of the experiment rather than a preliminary. Whatever is
introduced must be forced by the hostile world rather than selected because it resembles machinery that
exists elsewhere.

FIVE CANDIDATE REPRESENTATIONS are enumerated and none is chosen: explicit revocation, revision binding
via the already-implemented E1, expiry, ancestor revocation, and evidential staleness. Revision binding
gets the sharpest warning because it is cheapest to reach and would be the easiest mistake: a repair
exists precisely BECAUSE earlier work failed part-way, so the bytes probably HAVE moved, and binding
repair authority to unchanged bytes would likely refuse every legitimate repair. Evidential staleness is
where the other session's ancestry work would meet this one, and it is deliberately NOT preferred - it
is listed because a world may force it, not because the diagram suggests it.

SIX FROZEN WORLDS: invalidated authority refused; NOT-invalidated authority allowed, which is mandatory
because marking everything invalid would satisfy every other row; invalidation landing after install but
before the write, where governed-edit already says it resolves once and writes immediately so the window
is narrowed and not closed - a negative result there is acceptable and must be stated as an open window
rather than quietly repaired; an ANCESTOR grant invalidated rather than the leaf; a queue_task descendant
rather than a repair, so the property is not repair-specific; and a forged un-revocation. Four must-fire
controls, including one requiring the invalidation mechanism to be SHOWN to fire, or the refusals are
indistinguishable from a no-op, plus controls that Step 5's frozen widening result and Step 6's R1 and
SPEC all survive.

NOT DONE HERE: no re-opening of steps 5 or 6, no adoption of the evidential route for elegance, no fix
to the identifier debt - randomUUID sliced to 8 hex characters is 32 bits and its role changed in step 6
from locating a job to resolving an authority-bearing source, status new authority-bearing dependency
with collision properties UNESTABLISHED, not investigated and not replaced because there is no observed
failure - no flag enabled anywhere, and nothing inherited from the CONSOLIDATION-1 session.

The document also records the distinction ladder these steps produced, because a single field called
authorized true would have collapsed every rung: once granted, crossed this boundary, source still
exists, identifier resolves to the INTENDED source, source authority remains valid. Each rung was forced
by a hostile world rather than designed in advance.

Nothing in this document authorizes an implementation or a run.
