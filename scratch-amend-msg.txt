AMENDMENT 1: C4 fired, so W6 is vacuous at the chosen boundary - appended, not rewritten

The preregistration named the depth control as a live risk and required it be checked FIRST. It was,
and it failed. Recording that before any mechanism exists is the whole point of putting the control
first, and the frozen text above it is untouched.

A SUB-TASK CANNOT DELEGATE AT ALL. Not a depth limit - a categorical refusal in the sub-task loop at
agent.js:2778, which holds at any value of SUBTASK_MAX_DEPTH. So W6, a grandchild re-requesting what
the child narrowed away, is unreachable through spawn_subtask at any setting. The guard will NOT be
relaxed to reach it: loosening a load-bearing production guard so a world becomes observable is
weakening a control to obtain a result, and its own comment says this is the path that actually
recurses.

THE COMPOSING BOUNDARY IS THE QUEUE, AND IT FAILS THE OPPOSITE WAY. queue_task carries generation
parent plus one, capped by MAX_GENERATIONS 5, so a self-extending lineage of depth up to 5 is reachable
there. But only /start passes writeScope; both queue call sites pass none, so a queued run installs an
empty authority set and under the flag every write in it refuses. spawn_subtask gives a child too much,
ambiently and unrecorded, violating least-authority and accountability. The queue gives a descendant
nothing at all, violating non-loss - autonomy stops at the first write. Each boundary has the property
the other lacks, which is the same shape as the audit's finding about the two mechanisms, and it is now
measured rather than conjectured.

SO W6 SPLITS INTO TWO DIFFERENT KINDS OF EVIDENCE. W6a is algebraic - attenuate of attenuate is a
subset of attenuate - tested as a unit property of the function with no controller involved. W6b is
end-to-end composition across a real boundary, reachable only at the queue and blocked on how a queued
descendant gets any authority at all. The gap is named rather than papered over: W6a passing is NOT
evidence that authority composes in the live path, because a function with the right algebra installed
by a mechanism that overwrites shared state still loses the property.

AND ONE NEW FROZEN WORLD. W9: a run queues follow-up work and the queued descendant attempts a write
the parent was authorized for. The proposition requires something other than refuse-everything and
other than inherit-silently, and today there is no answer - the descendant gets no authority, and the
only alternatives currently representable are ambient inheritance or owner enumeration, which cannot
cover discovered work. W9 is where attenuation would have to prove itself and where the hypothesis is
actually load-bearing.

Unchanged: the proposition, the five candidate representations, controls C1 to C3, and worlds W1 to W5,
W7 and W8. Nothing implemented, no representation chosen, flag still off.
