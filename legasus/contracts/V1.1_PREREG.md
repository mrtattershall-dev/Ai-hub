# Contract v1.1 — observation attribution. Predictions frozen before the emitter changed.
2026-09-21. v1.0.0 is **preserved unmodified** as the contract steps 2–4 were actually run against.

## What v1.1 adds, and where

`measurement.observation.attribution`, required as a key, `string` or `null`. It sits **inside the
observation**, not at certificate level: six observations from six producers must not silently
inherit one global attribution.

    observation { ref, status-bearing evidential_force, procedure, attribution, context }

## The frozen rule

    The adapter MAY transmit attribution and MAY validate its presence and shape.
    The adapter MAY NOT synthesize it, and MAY NOT infer it from procedure, filename,
    producer, or certificate issuer.
    attribution: null  ->  measurement->OBSERVE is non-mintable.
    ATTRIBUTION NEVER CONSTITUTES AUTHORITY.

provenance check it does not have).

## Rules

v1.0.0 is not edited. v1.0 fixtures are not regenerated. Attribution values are supplied by the
producer from the real binding record and are not invented by the emitter for convenience. If V-4
fails, the measurement domain stays empty and is reported as such — the contract gaining the word
does not entitle the bridge to the correspondence.
