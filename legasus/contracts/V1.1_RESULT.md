## The first real mint across the seam

`observe()` called with v1.1 provenance returns a token that passes `isAuthority()`, is `EPISTEMIC`,
and carries an **empty grant**. Evidence establishes what may be believed and originates no
permission. That is the calculus minting on its own terms from foreign data, which is what the whole
seam was for.

## V-5 is the result worth keeping

Adding a field to a contract usually just makes a check pass. Here it converted a **vocabulary gap**
into a **genuine divergence**, and the divergence is informative:

    M gate       asks about instrument CAPABILITY for a class of observation
    observe()    asks about provenance completeness for THIS observation

A certificate with capability, evidential force, and `attribution: null` makes production permit and
the calculus refuse on `PROVENANCE_INCOMPLETE`. Both sides can now express the concept, so this is
not a gap to be reported as UNMAPPABLE — it is two different questions disagreeing, which is exactly
what the bridge apparatus exists to make visible. Predicted before running.
