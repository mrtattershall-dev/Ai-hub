# BIND-CJS step 15 — make the self-report observation risk being wrong (frozen 2026-09-21 08:15)

Across steps 12 and 14, five attacks succeeded through subject-controlled descriptive fields or
channel artifacts. That is a **retrospective regularity**. Under the charter it earns nothing
until it predicts a failure it has not already explained. This expedition is that prediction.

## The structural prediction, frozen before the world exists

> For a newly selected, previously unattacked proposition whose affirmative decider depends on a
> **subject-controlled descriptive field**, a world can be constructed in which the subject emits
> the expected field value while the proposition is false, and the frozen decider will affirm.

The six required steps, all completed **before** any hostile world was built:

1. **Proposition selected independently of whether the attack works** — see below, and note that
   it was chosen because it is load-bearing for BIND, not because it looked fragile.
2. **The exact field the decider consumes** — a line matching `^PASS <id>` on stdout.
3. **The subject controls that field** — the witness prints its own case lines.
4. **The false-world prediction** — frozen as R1.
5. **Genuine positive and negative controls** — included.
6. **Built once.**

## The proposition

    P8: witness case <id> passed.

**Why this one.** It is not a corner of the apparatus: BIND-1's entire case-level granularity,
its 93-case matrix, and every per-case klass in steps 1-14 rest on witnesses printing their own
`PASS|FAIL` lines. If P8 falls, it falls in the load-bearing place. It has not been attacked in
any previous expedition.

**The decider, frozen:** `D-P8(id) = stdout contains a line matching ^PASS <id>`.

**The oracle.** The experimenter needs to know the underlying truth independently of the field
under test, so the witness also writes the *actual* result of its assertion to a separate oracle
channel that **the decider never reads**. This is the same asymmetry every earlier expedition
used — I knew the impostor was not M — stated explicitly here.

## The worlds

    CTL-PASS    assertion true,  prints PASS      decider must affirm
    CTL-FAIL    assertion false, prints FAIL      decider must decline
    R1-FORGED   assertion FALSE, prints PASS      representation true, event false
    R2-LOST     assertion TRUE,  line written then the process exits abruptly
                                                  representation false, event true

## Predictions

**R1.** `D-P8` affirms in R1-FORGED, where the case did not pass.
FALSIFIER: it declines. **If it declines, the self-report pattern's first prospective test has
failed and the pattern is not reinterpreted afterwards.**

**R2.** `D-P8` declines in R2-LOST, where the case did pass.
This half is **genuinely uncertain**: whether an abrupt exit loses a written stdout line on this
platform is an empirical question, not a foregone conclusion. If the line survives, R2 is
falsified and only the forward direction stands.

**R-CTL.** The two controls behave, or the expedition is void rather than informative.

## What this expedition may not do

- It may not rename the pattern, or promote it to a principle, on a single prospective success.
  One landed prediction changes the pattern's *epistemic status*, not its rank.
- It may not repair BIND's case-level granularity, propose a second channel, or add a field.
  If P8 falls, the consequence for steps 1-14 is **recorded**, not fixed.
- It may not conclude that the five earlier attacks share a mechanism. That is the **next**
  question — whether "self-report / channel artifact" groups one thing or several unrelated
  failures after the fact — and it needs its own experiment.
- Mechanism, interpretation and every earlier decider stay untouched.

## The consequence that must be stated whatever happens

If R1 lands, then every per-case outcome in BIND-1 and BIND-2 — 3162 records, 604
discriminations, 568 edges — rests on a field the subject controls. That would **not** invalidate
those results: the witnesses there were not adversarial, and the mutants were generated by
Legasus rather than by the subject. But the entitlement would change from *established* to
*conditional on the witness not misreporting*, and that condition has never been stated in any of
those records. Saying so is part of this result, not a follow-up.
