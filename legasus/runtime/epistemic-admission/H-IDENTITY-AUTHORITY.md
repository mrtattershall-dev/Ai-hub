# H-IDENTITY-AUTHORITY — recorded as a hypothesis, standing NONE
2026-09-21. Entered in the ledger, **not adopted**.

> **Authority may transfer across identities only when the equivalence or relation connecting those
> identities is itself established.**

## Why it is worth recording

It has been reached independently from five directions in this branch, each of which began as a
separate defect:

    witness identity     a relation NAME satisfied an obligation; the relation INSTANCE had to be
                         bound to this claim's domain, this derivation's premises, and real evidence
    evidence identity    an evidence_root that merely EXISTED; it had to resolve to authority
                         establishing that exact relation claim
    world identity       authority transferred across repositories and populations; the minimal
                         identity was measured to be {repository, claim_domain}
    rule identity        a rule's meaning changed while its fingerprint did not; identity had to
                         cover the semantic dependency closure
    referent identity    the justification graph's own older lesson: a name collision is not an
                         admission

Five arrivals at one shape is why it is in the ledger. It is **not** why it would be adopted — that
is exactly the retrospective-convergence argument that keeps H-SUBST unadopted after seven instances.

## What would make it standing, and what would refute it

**Standing** requires a prospective prediction it makes that a shallower account does not, tested on
evidence that did not exist when it was written.

**Refutation** is cheaper and is the thing to watch for: *a transfer that is legitimate and cannot be
justified through established identity or relation evidence.* The next milestone — natural relation
production, where ordinary producer work emits a relation claim rather than a test retargeting one —
is the most likely place to find one. If real producer output forces such a transfer, that is far
more informative than another internally clean confirmation.
