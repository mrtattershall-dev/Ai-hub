# Unattached governance — U1..U7. Frozen 2026-09-21, before any policy code exists.

## The gap G5 left open

G5 made an unattached obligation **visible**. It did not prevent its **consequence**. Today an
unmatched `DESIGNATED` entry is reported while its intended consumer may succeed through a
substitute under the unnamed default — the system has made the failure legible without stopping it.
U1 demonstrates that before anything is changed.

## The hard part, frozen before choosing a policy

The three candidate policies are **invalidate the merge**, **block the affected admissions**, or
**diagnose only**. The middle one looks like the proportionate choice and **it is not computable
here**:

> An unresolved governance entry denotes **nothing in this merged set**. There is no record it
> points at, so there is no way to determine *which* consumers it was meant to govern. The affected
> set is unknown, not merely unenumerated.

So `BLOCK` may not be implemented as an approximation. **If requested, it is refused, naming why.**
Building a plausible-looking `BLOCK` that blocks whatever seems nearby would be the invention of a
distinction reality has not supplied, and it would read as precision.

## The policy chosen, and who authorizes it

**Default: `INVALIDATE`.** An unattached obligation means the governor intended a burden that was
not applied. Proceeding under a weaker default is an unauthorized reduction of that burden — the
same defect A1 refuses on the request channel, arriving through absence instead of through asking.
Failing closed is the only option that does not quietly substitute a burden.

**`DIAGNOSE` exists and requires explicit authorization from the governor**, recorded in the result
as an authorized downgrade. It is never the default and never selectable by the requester: the
policy lives in the **governing** channel, exactly like the mode (A1, T7). U4 and U6 attack this.

**Consequence, accepted deliberately:** three arms of the governance-binding suite (G1, G2, G5)
observed detection under what is now `DIAGNOSE`. They will state that policy explicitly. Their
required observations do not change; only the policy under which they observe becomes named. That
is disclosed here rather than discovered in a diff.

## The arms

| arm | required observation |
|---|---|
| **U1** the consequence today | with diagnosis only, an unmatched `DESIGNATED` is reported **while its intended consumer succeeds through a substitute**. The failure is visible and not prevented |
| **U2** the affected set is not computable | two consumers are indistinguishable from an unresolved entry: nothing in the entry can say which one it meant |
| **U3** `INVALIDATE` is the default | unresolved governance produces **no admissions at all**, naming every unattached entry |
| **U4** `DIAGNOSE` needs authorization | available only when the governor selects it, and recorded in the result as an authorized downgrade rather than as normal operation |
| **U5** `BLOCK` is refused, not faked | requesting it refuses, naming that the affected set is not computable. No approximation is built |
| **U6** policy is governed, not requested | a requested policy is recorded and never decides — identical admission projection to not asking |
| **U7** `subjectOf` boundary | the helper prevents missing-subject and coordinate-only assertions; it does **not** establish that it resolved the *right* occurrence. An **independently constructed** expected occurrence is required, and an arm must fail when the helper returns a neighbour |

## Predictions, committed now

- **U1 will demonstrate the gap.** I expect the substitute path to succeed under the default while
  the unattached `DESIGNATED` sits in the report.
- **U2** I expect to hold trivially and to be worth asserting anyway, because it is the reason
  `BLOCK` cannot exist: the arm records *why* the proportionate-looking policy is unavailable.
- **U3, U4, U6** I expect to hold once the policy is in the governing channel.
- **U5** I expect to hold by construction; its value is that the refusal is a refusal and not a
  silent degradation to `DIAGNOSE`.
- **U7 is the one I expect to be uncomfortable.** `subjectOf` is my own apparatus, and an arm that
  tests the apparatus with the apparatus proves nothing. The expected occurrence must be computed
  from the entry by `occurrenceOf` independently of any outcome, and a mutant returning a neighbour
  must die.

No lineage identity — that is the next preregistration, and the question there is **who may
establish continuity**, not how to generate a name. No registry rules added or changed (still
**3 authored, 0/15**). No strength lattice. No exhaustion mechanism. `COMPLETE` stays unsatisfied.
**S6 stays preserved and untouched.** No freshness rule. No repair of the F2 boundary. No
approximated `BLOCK`.
