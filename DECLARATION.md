# The Legasus Declaration

**Version 1.0.0** · adopted 2026-09-29 · author: tatte (Micheal)

> **Amendment rule, which this document applies to itself.** The text below is not edited in place. A
> later version is added beneath it and supersedes it by addition; every prior version stays readable
> exactly as written. If Legasus eventually earns a different philosophy, the old declaration remains
> part of its own legacy — which is the only way this document could be consistent with what it says.

## The text — v1.0.0

Legasus exists to carry what is proven forward.

We believe intelligence should not destroy the foundation it inherits. It should study it, respect it,
test it, strengthen it, and leave it better than it found it.

Too much software is created by momentum: generated, patched, overwritten, and trusted because it looks
finished. Legasus stands for a different standard. No claim becomes truth because an AI said it. No
change earns permanence without evidence. No failure disappears just because a later attempt passed.

Legasus is Legacy + Pegasus: the strength to preserve what matters, and the wings to rise beyond present
limitations.

Its ivory foundation represents the working knowledge already earned. Its golden core represents
verified truth. Its branching wings represent intelligence that can explore many paths without losing
the one that already works.

Our purpose is to make AI-generated software dependable — not by demanding blind trust in smarter
models, but by giving intelligence discipline: observation before assumption, authority before action,
verification before commitment, and restoration when the evidence says a change should not survive.

We do not worship the output.
We prove it.
We do not rewrite what works.
We repair with precision.
We do not erase failure.
We learn from it.
We do not build forward by breaking what came before.

**Legasus: Build forward without breaking what came before.**

## Interpretive notes accompanying v1.0.0

These are notes *about* the text, recorded alongside it. They do not amend it. Authored by tatte on
adoption, because two of them resolve a real ambiguity that would otherwise contradict working
classifications already in use.

### 1. "We do not rewrite what works. We repair with precision."

**Read this as: preserve established BEHAVIOUR, not the old IMPLEMENTATION.**

Without that reading it eventually contradicts `SUPERSEDED` — a classification already in active use in
`CONSOLIDATION-1_MANIFEST.md`, where a later mechanism legitimately replaces an earlier one. Legasus
must be able to replace old code when it can establish that the replacement **carries the old
obligations** and improves something legitimately measurable.

The deeper form:

> **Preserve the legacy of what was proven, not necessarily the mechanism that first proved it.**

That is what makes "Legacy + Pegasus" cohere. Legacy without wings becomes technical debt. Wings
without legacy become destructive generation. Legasus carries the established obligations upward while
letting the implementation beneath them evolve.

### 2. "Legasus exists to carry what is proven forward."

The load-bearing word is **proven** — not *old*, and not *every historical implementation*. What
survives should be what the evidence established.

### 3. "No claim becomes truth because an AI said it."

The most fundamental sentence here, and the one with the most machinery behind it: the representation
experiments, the forged-PASS findings, the faithful-observer failure, and the three-column distinction
between test-only machinery and live product machinery all orbit this single problem.

### 4. "No failure disappears just because a later attempt passed."

Increasingly an engineering requirement rather than a philosophical one. Preserved experimental nulls,
failed attempts kept in the corpus, stale ancestry, refusal records and repair provenance are all
implementations of this line.

### 5. What the declaration deliberately does NOT say

It does not make Legasus about small models. A 1.5B, a 7B, or a future enormous model can sit inside
it unchanged, because the principle is about the relationship rather than the size:

> **Intelligence proposes; evidence determines what gets to become reality.**
