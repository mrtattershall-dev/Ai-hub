P6 AMENDMENT 1: a sixth rung, seven meanings of invalid, and what the finding costs the word authority

Appended; the frozen proposition, the reachability finding, the six worlds and the four controls are
untouched. No implementation and no run.

A SIXTH RUNG. The ladder was one short: authority valid WHEN CHECKED is not authority valid WHEN THE
EFFECT OCCURS. That rung is new and unestablished, and it reframes Q3 from a hard edge case into the
rung's own experiment - P6 may establish validity at RESOLUTION while establishing nothing about
validity at EFFECT, and those must be reported as different guarantees. Q3 is explicitly NOT to be
closed by checking twice, which yields check, check again, still a window, effect: that moves the
window rather than closing it. Closing it may eventually need validation and effect to be one atomic
authority-consuming operation, or an explicitly bounded and stated race, and both are downstream
speculation the frozen experiment decides first.

SEVEN MEANINGS OF INVALID, NOT FIVE, and the framing matters more than the count: these are different
propositions about WHY permission ceases to be justified, not interchangeable implementations of
revocation. Two are new to the record. The permitted operation was ALREADY CONSUMED, which is E4
single-use and unimplemented. And the world changed such that the original scope no longer corresponds
to the INTENDED OBJECT - referential drift, where the grant still names a.py but a.py is no longer the
thing it named. That last one is the same shape as the identifier dependency step 6 created: a grant
pins a path, a continuation pins a queue id, the NAME survives while what it denotes may not, and
nothing currently checks the correspondence. Recorded as a connection rather than investigated, since
no misresolution has been observed. None of the seven is adopted.

AND WHAT THE FIRST FINDING COSTS THE WORD AUTHORITY, stated plainly because it is the most consequential
line in the document: Legasus currently has authority issuance, transport, attenuation and source
continuity, and no reachable lifecycle in which issued authority becomes invalid. The lifecycle is
issued, crossed, attenuated, continued, valid indefinitely - unless the source item itself disappears,
which step 6 handles, and which is SOURCE CONTINUITY rather than authority freshness. Every use of the
word authority in this project's records should be read against that limit until P6 changes it.

The next session on this line begins by trying to make each candidate definition of invalidity
contradict reality, not by coding.
