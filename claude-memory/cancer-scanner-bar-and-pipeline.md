The decisive experiment, unchanged: freeze LegaScreen, point it at code whose defect nobody
knows, have it find a real one using a general invariant frozen before the defect was known,
and have an independent diagnosis agree. Checklist as of 2026-09-21: generic invariants,
prospective internal defects, private-code access, external transfer (TRANSFER-2), witness
discovery (BIND-1 DISCOVER), generic perturbation, discrimination, independent replay
(BIND-2), false-equivalence rejection (4/7 clusters split) — all done; unknown external live
defect and its independent confirmation — NOT done.

Emerging shape (hypothesis): effect discovery + DISCOVER locate where to look; BIND says what
existing witnesses can/cannot distinguish; REPLAY tests whether apparent relations survive an
independent view; LegaScreen then challenges justified relations -> HELD / VIOLATED / UNKNOWN.
Candidate screening signal: UNASSERTED (output changed, witness executed, witness didn't
care) reported as UNSUPPORTED_BEHAVIORAL_DISTINCTION with no defect claim. Count it per
behavioural class (17 of 21 in BIND-2), never per record (272) — one mutant contributed 71.
Possible simplification: LegaScreen ("what violates the map?") and SUPERSEDE ("what must a
replacement preserve?") as two consumers of one support/evidence map. Not canonized.

**Why:** keeps the bar from drifting down after a good night, and keeps the diagram from
becoming architecture by elegance.
**How to apply:** no "scanner works" claim until both unmet items are met externally. See
[[bind-2-support-result]], [[odysseus-foreign-evidence-test]].
