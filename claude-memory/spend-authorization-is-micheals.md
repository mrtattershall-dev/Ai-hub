2026-09-25: a $15 figure and a "Fable can proceed" arrived from a non-authorizing voice, which then withdrew its own standing ("my $5 figure was a suggested cap, not spending authorization from Micheal... keep Modal off until he authorized the run; silence doesn't change that"). Micheal then authorized $5 in answer to that exact question. Ran on $5, not $15.

**Why:** a larger number offered by someone without authority is not headroom. Standing rule: no paid compute without an authorized cap, and the authorization has to come from Micheal.

**How to apply:** record the authorization — who, how much, what terms — inside the frozen definition and COMMIT IT before deploying. If two figures are in play, use the one Micheal actually answered. See [[test-feedback-was-unreachable]] for the run it gated.
