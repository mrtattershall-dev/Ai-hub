2026-09-29, tatte, after the branch survey. The bottleneck moved and the reporting should follow.

**Do not say the epistemics are "done."** They are mature enough that continuing to build isolated
epistemic machinery has diminishing value, because the biggest unknown moved upward:

> Does the assembled system preserve the properties its components established separately?

P6 alone shows the ontology is still open. What changed is the ranking, not the completeness.

**The value thesis is now measured, and it is the original one.** OVERNIGHT-1: 19 vs 18 repairs is
no evidence of increased repair capability; 0 vs 11 regressions and 2172 vs 4678 task-seconds are
substantial. The architecture's value is *not making the model smarter — making its intelligence
safer and less wasteful*. That is more interesting than one more repair, and it is the argument for
assembling now.

**Never merge the laboratory into main.** Build one candidate head from committed evidence, resolve
competing implementations there, then run ONE ordinary launch through the whole conjunction — no
benchmark-only environment facts, no manually supplied authority a real client could not send.
Expect it to fail; the first failure exposes what isolated testing could not. Only a clean pass
earns the name PRODUCT-0, then the game milestone, and only then main.

**Related:** [[standing-report-reporting-rules]], [[legasus-phase1-integration-done]],
[[hub-location-and-coord]]. The project's own standing rule agrees and is recorded in commit
678013f5: do not inherit component evidence as evidence of system composition.
