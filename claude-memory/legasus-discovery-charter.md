Legasus is not an established framework being applied. Its underlying epistemology is being
experimentally discovered at the same time as the system is built. There is no assumption that
a ready-made abstraction exists elsewhere for the distinctions being uncovered (requested ≠
served ≠ executed; intervention membership; evidence cardinality; positional destruction of
execution evidence). Some will have analogues in testing, formal methods, distributed systems,
observability, provenance or experimental design; some are old ideas renamed; the composition
may be novel. **The experiments decide what Legasus requires first; literature comparison comes
later and does not get to pre-empt the finding.**

The working loop, in tatte's words: *what proposition did Legasus just claim, what evidence
entitled that claim, and can we construct a world where that entitlement becomes invalid?*
Then attack it. If it survives, its authority grows. If it fails, preserve the failure. **If
the failure exposes a distinction the architecture cannot represent, do NOT invent the
abstraction — construct another experiment where that distinction matters.** Only after reality
repeatedly demands it does it earn a place in Legasus.

**Why:** tonight's findings (artifact ≠ producer; resolution ≠ evaluation ≠ intervention;
M M S M M certified VALID) were all DISCOVERED, not anticipated. Evaluation multiplicity was
not known to matter when the night began. An abstraction promoted for explaining history rather
than predicting a new failure would have pre-empted every one of them.

**Correction (2026-09-21, tatte):** reality does not decide which architectures may EXIST - a
builder can construct almost anything. Reality decides what a construction is ENTITLED TO
CLAIM. The last-observation classifier was not forbidden; it was built, and then falsified by
M M S M M. So the work converges on CONSTRAINTS, not on one true architecture: whatever you
build must carry the evidentiary burden of the authority you give it. A completely different
machine that survives the same hostile worlds is equally legitimate - and would be strong
evidence that the findings are deeper than this implementation. **Build anything. Prove what it
gets to claim.**

**The descent rule (2026-09-21, tatte):** depth is not evidence. There are unlimited ways to
construct a deeper explanation after seeing failures. **Every descent must purchase its depth
with a prediction the previous layer could not make.** Otherwise stop digging and preserve
UNKNOWN. This is what stops "go deeper" from becoming an excuse to invent increasingly
beautiful explanations - the H-DEFAULT / H-LOSS / H-DISTINCTION lesson, generalised.

**The discrimination rule (earned 2026-09-21, one experiment):** a prospective prediction can
still be NON-DISCRIMINATING. A deeper explanation earns standing only by predicting an outcome
a materially shallower explanation does not predict equally well. Earned by BIND-CJS step 16
(commit 097a99e / result in legasus/out/discriminate): step 15's forged-PASS result was
predicted equally by "a program can emit false text" and by the deeper account, so it purchased
nothing; step 16 built two worlds where the two accounts predicted OPPOSITE outcomes, and the
deeper one won both - decisively in the half that was not definitional (a separate, faithful
reporter affirmed a false case). One experiment. The rule is recorded, not canonised.

**How to apply:** freeze a minimum expectation only; never pre-name the missing distinction;
record raw evidence at full cardinality; report what the current representation says AND what it
discards. See [[bind-2-support-result]], [[cancer-scanner-bar-and-pipeline]],
[[transfer-bind-cannot-attach]].
