## The claim

A projection `P` is **sufficient** for a proposition `Q` exactly when

    P(a) = P(b)   =>   a ~_Q b            SOUNDNESS: never collapse a distinction Q depends on
    a ~_Q b       =>   P(a) = P(b)        MINIMALITY: never preserve one Q does not

where `a ~_Q b` means substituting one for the other cannot change the justified answer to Q. Two
error directions follow: **illegitimate collapse** and **illegitimate separation**.

**NO INVENTION IS CLAIMED.** This is contextual/observational equivalence and intervention-based
identification of relevant variables, reached from a different direction. The prior art is older and
better developed than anything here; what is being tested is whether the framing predicts something
about THIS system that was not already known.

## THE LOCATING RULE, FIXED BEFORE ANY DEFECT IS LOOKED AT

This is the whole difference between a theory and a frame that fits everything.

> A collapse or separation may be located **only in a PROJECTION** - a step that produces a
> representation consumed downstream. It may **NOT** be located in a consumer's decision procedure.

If a consumer that had the distinction available simply failed to branch on it, that is
**NEITHER**, not a collapse. Without this rule every defect can be narrated as "the consumer treated
two cases alike", and the taxonomy becomes unfalsifiable. The classifier refuses an entry that
cannot name the projection step.
